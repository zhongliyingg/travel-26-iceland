## DO SOON — 1–2 Months Out

- **Friðheimar lunch** — Nov 20 · tomato greenhouse restaurant on the Golden Circle · book ahead at fridheimar.is · **book ~14:00** (last stop of the revised loop, after Gullfoss) · if that is too late to eat, book ~12:45 instead and move Gullfoss to after lunch
- **Buy Yaktrax ice cleats** — **4 pairs, one per person** · the single highest-value item for a November trip with 70+ travellers: a slip is the realistic trip-ending risk · choose the easy on/off strap type (they cannot be worn indoors or on coaches) · needed at Gullfoss, Geysir, Kerið, Diamond Beach and especially the Sólheimajökull gravel path

## CLOSER TO DATE — October / November

- **Pack waterproof layers** for everyone: windproof jacket, trousers, gloves, warm hat, merino base layers
- **Bookmark road.is** and download **112 Iceland app** — check road conditions every morning before driving
- **Blue Car drop-off** — Nov 22 at 05:00–05:30 · Blue Car Rental Iceland, Blikavöllur 3, Keflavík (~5 min from the Courtyard) · office opens 05:00 in winter · fill the tank and load the bags the night before · drop everyone and the bags at KEF departures first, then the driver returns the car and walks back to the terminal (~3–6 min across the car park, Yaktrax on) — the airport shuttle may not start until 06:00
- **Confirm Skógar Museum hours** — Nov 19 · listed as open daily 10:00–16:00 Nov–Apr at skogasafn.is · no booking needed, but re-check before the drive
- **Whales of Iceland** — Nov 13 early afternoon, before the Lava Show · open daily 10:00–17:00 year-round · adult 4,000 ISK (family ticket covers 2 adults + 2 children only) · **buy at the door, not online**: senior discounts are only given when booked onsite · live guided tour daily at 10:30 and 13:30 — arrive ~13:15 to buy tickets and join the 13:30 one · Fiskislóð 23–25, Grandi · re-check hours at whalesoficeland.is before going
- **Confirm Þingvellir Hakið car park access** — Nov 20 · upper visitor centre lot gives the paved, step-free route into the rift · parking fee payable online at parka.is
- **Exchange cash** — total ≈ SGD 275–480 (approx. rates: 1 EUR ≈ SGD 1.50 · 1 SGD ≈ ISK 97 — re-check before exchanging) · **EUR €150–250 ≈ SGD 225–375** in small notes + coins, for Frankfurt (Christmas market stalls, Glühwein mug deposits, Sachsenhausen taverns, toilets, tips) — exchange at a Singapore money changer · **ISK 5,000–10,000 ≈ SGD 50–105** (optional), for Kolaportið flea market — ATM in Iceland, don't exchange in Singapore · everything else in Iceland is card; fuel pumps need a chip-and-PIN card
- **Prepaid fuel card (backup)** — Nov 16, before leaving Reykjavík · South Coast pumps are mostly unstaffed and need a **chip-and-PIN card**; foreign cards are sometimes declined, and cash can't be used at the pump · confirm the PIN for at least one card before the trip · as a backup, buy an **N1 prepaid card** (ISK 10,000 ≈ SGD 105) inside a staffed N1 station · it only works at N1 pumps (stations at Hvolsvöllur, Vík and Kirkjubæjarklaustur on your route) · unused balance usually isn't refunded, so don't overbuy · total fuel for the 5 driving days is roughly ISK 25,000–30,000 ≈ SGD 260–310
