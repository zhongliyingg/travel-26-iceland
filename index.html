Nov13FriDay 3
Reykjavík
Viking history, Whales of Iceland & Lava Show
🏨 Reykjavík apartment (night 2 of 4) · 🚶 On foot + short taxis

Viking history in the morning, then Grandi harbour in the afternoon — Whales of Iceland and the Lava Show are on the same street, ~10 min walk apart — and Reykjavík Kitchen for dinner. Mostly indoors: an easy first full day.

Breakfast
Kaffivagninn ~08:45–09:30 · Grandagarður 10, Old Harbour · ~10 min flat walk from the apartment
Iceland's oldest restaurant (since 1935), right on the harbour — a warm, unhurried first breakfast with a view of the boats. Listed as open daily from 08:00 (older listings say weekdays from 07:30), so confirm hours. It is still dark until ~10:00 and the harbour paths can be icy — Yaktrax on. Backup: Grái Kötturinn (Hverfisgata 16, ~5–8 min walk, listed 08:00–14:00).
Morning
National Museum of Iceland Optional · 10:00 · ~5 min by taxi
Viking age artefacts at Iceland's national museum. Skip it if energy is low — the Settlement Exhibition tells the Viking story in a more compact way.
Late morning
Settlement Exhibition Aðalstræti 16 · ~3 min walk from the apartment
Walk-over glass floors reveal actual Viking longhouse ruins beneath the city.
Lunch
Grandi Mathöll Grandi harbour · ~5 min by taxi
A casual food hall on the Grandi harbourfront — several stalls, so everyone can pick. A few minutes from Whales of Iceland.
Early afternoon
Whales of Iceland Fiskislóð 23–25, Grandi · arrive ~13:15 · allow ~1 hour
🐋 Life-size models of over 20 whale species in one flat, indoor hall — easy going for all ages. Buy tickets at the door (senior discounts are only given onsite) and join the free live guided tour at 13:30. Open 10:00–17:00.
Mid-afternoon
Free time in Grandi ~14:30–15:45
The Lava Show only runs every 2 hours (10:00, 12:00, 14:00, 16:00…), so there's a gap after the Whales tour. Find a warm café on the harbour, take a short walk along the boats, or sit down somewhere if anyone's tired.
Late afternoon
Lava Show, Grandi Harbour Fiskislóð 73 · ~10 min walk from Whales of Iceland · ✅ Booked · 16:00 show (arrive by 15:40) · 50–75 min
The world's only live lava experience, where real molten rock is superheated to 1,100°C and poured into the showroom. You see it flow, feel the heat on your face and hear it hiss and cool. If your ticket is Classic, ask on arrival about upgrading to Premium for balcony seats, a drink on arrival, a backstage furnace tour and a giftwrapped piece of lava to take home. Exactly what 王安宇 arranged for the 花少5 cast on their city day here.
After the show
Krónan Grandi Grocery shop · ~17:15–17:45 · Fiskislóð, Grandi · a few minutes from the Lava Show
The big Reykjavík shop — Krónan's Grandi store is larger and cheaper than anything downtown, and you are already here. Buy breakfast for Days 4–6 (Day 6 starts with a 07:00 car pickup, before anything opens), food for the Day 4 early dinner at the apartment, a backup dinner in case the Day 5 aurora retry happens, snacks for the aurora tour, and car snacks for the Day 6 drive. Then take a taxi home with the bags. Confirm the address and hours on kronan.is.
Evening
Reykjavík Kitchen Dinner · ✅ Booked · 20:00 · ~5 min by taxi
Classic Icelandic cooking — try the lamb shank and Arctic char. Taxi back to the apartment after the Krónan shop (~17:45) to rest first.

Nov14SatDay 4
Reykjavík
Perlan + Northern Lights tour
🏨 Reykjavík apartment (night 3 of 4) · 🚕 Taxi to Perlan (~5–10 min) · 🚶 Walk to Tour Bus Stop 5 (Harpa) for the bus tour

Perlan first thing (booked for 10:00), a light afternoon — the Kolaportið flea market or simply a rest — then an early dinner and the 8:30pm Northern Lights tour.

Morning
Perlan ✅ Booked · 10:00 entry · ~5–10 min by taxi (leave ~09:45) · allow 2–3 hours
The iconic glass-domed museum on Öskjuhlíð hill. Explore the 100m indoor ice cave (built from 400 tons of real ice and snow), a Forces of Nature volcano exhibit, and the Áróra planetarium — Iceland's only northern lights show using 8K projection and 7.1 surround sound. A guaranteed, breathtaking indoor aurora — and the perfect primer for tonight. The 360° observation deck gives panoramic views over the entire city. Sunrise is around 10:00, so start indoors with the ice cave, exhibits and Áróra show, and save the observation deck for around 11:30 in daylight. Have lunch at Perlan's café before leaving (~12:30).
Afternoon
Rest, or Kolaportið flea market Optional · back at the apartment after lunch
Keep the day light — tonight is a late one. Whales of Iceland has moved to Day 3, alongside the Lava Show.
🛍 Kolaportið flea market · Tryggvagata 19, next door to the apartment · open Saturdays 11:00–17:00 · lopapeysa sweaters, Icelandic foodstuffs and secondhand finds. Easy to drop into for 30 minutes after a rest. It is also listed on Day 11 (another Saturday), so pick whichever day suits.
Early evening
Dinner ~17:45 · at the apartment or nearby in the Old Harbour
Cook something simple and warm at the apartment, or book a table near the Old Harbour for 17:45–18:00. Be back home by 19:00 to layer up.
Evening
Northern Lights bus tour ✅ Booked · 8:30pm departure · pickup at Tour Bus Stop 5, Harpa Concert Hall (Faxagata) · ~6–8 min walk
Be at the stop by 20:00 (pickup starts up to 30 minutes before departure) — leave the apartment ~19:50. The bus returns to the same stop around 00:30. Hot chocolate included, and a free re-try on cloudy nights (valid for 3 years) — if no lights tonight, rebook for Nov 15. Dress for standing still in sub-zero wind: thermals, hat, gloves, warm boots; bring snacks.

Reykjavík
Last day in the city — on foot
🏨 Reykjavík apartment (night 4 of 4) · 🚶 On foot

A slow start after last night's aurora tour, a gentle morning loop round Tjörnin, then an afternoon walk downhill from Hallgrímskirkja to the harbour — no driving needed. Keep the evening open: an aurora retry if Nov 14 drew a blank, otherwise a farewell dinner at Grillmarkaðurinn.

Morning
Slow start at the apartment
Sleep in after getting back ~00:30 from the aurora tour, then a relaxed breakfast. Sunday shops on Laugavegur mostly open late anyway.
Late morning
Tjörnin pond ~10:30 · ~5–8 min flat walk from the apartment
A flat loop around the pond, past City Hall — soft light just after sunrise (~10:10), often partly frozen, with swans and ducks. Paths can be icy in the morning — Yaktrax on.
Lunch
Café Loki ~12:00 · Lokastígur 28, opposite Hallgrímskirkja · ~5 min taxi up the hill from Tjörnin (~1.2 km uphill on foot)
Traditional Icelandic food: lamb meat soup, plokkfiskur (fish stew), rye bread ice cream.
Early afternoon
Hallgrímskirkja ~13:00 · right across the road
Reykjavík's landmark church — take the lift up the tower for the city view. Midday is the best daylight of a November day. Sunday 15 Nov hours: open 10:00–17:00, church and tower closed ~11:30–12:30 for the 11:00 Mass, last tower admission 16:45.
Afternoon
Skólavörðustígur & Laugavegur ~13:45 · Walk downhill
Skólavörðustígur, the rainbow street of boutiques and galleries, leads down to Laugavegur, the main shopping street — 66°North, wool sweaters, a café break. Shops are open by now, though Sunday hours are shorter than weekdays.
🥖 Optional soup stop: Svarta Kaffið (Laugavegur 54) — soup served in a bread bowl · walk-in only, no reservations · check access for the 70+ travellers.
Dusk
Harpa & Old Harbour ~16:00 · ~10 min walk from the bottom of Laugavegur · sunset ~16:15 · short walk home
Walk through the glass concert hall, then along the Old Harbour waterfront.
Evening
Grillmarkaðurinn (KIV) Dinner · 20:00 · Lækjargata 2a · ~5 min walk · only if there is no aurora retry tonight
A splurge-worthy farewell-to-Reykjavík dinner. Tables are only available from 20:00. Book ahead, but check you can cancel on the morning of Nov 15 if the retry is needed.
CultureFoodShopping
💡 Tax refund tip: Most stores on Laugavegur offer a VAT refund for visitors who live outside Iceland. Minimum spend ISK 12,000 (~SGD 125) per receipt — items on the same receipt count together. Ask for a Tax-Free form at the till, then claim at Keflavík Airport before checking in your bags — you must show the goods, so keep them in your carry-on.
🌌 Aurora retry window: If the Nov 14 tour drew a blank, contact your operator this morning to book your free retry for tonight — it's your last night in the city. Pickup is ~20:00 at Harpa, so skip Grillmarkaðurinn and eat early near the Old Harbour instead. From Day 6 you're in the dark countryside, where you can watch for the lights from your hotel.

The 花少5 South Coast — stops 1 to 4
🏨 Kirkjubæjarklaustur cabin (night 1 of 3) · 🚗 Pick up car at Blue Car Rental, Grandi (07:00) · ~255 km · ~3h 20min driving

Pick up the car at Blue Car Rental (Fiskislóð 22, Grandi) at 07:00, load the bags at the apartment and leave by 08:30. Follow the 花少5 South Coast route in order — stops 1 to 4 today. Stop 5 (Dyrhólaey Lighthouse) is on Day 9, the first stop on the drive west to Selfoss — a second chance at calm weather. Then continue ~1h east to your base for the next three nights.

Stop 1
Seljalandsfoss ~1h 45min from Reykjavík
Walk the full circuit around the falls. Do NOT go behind — the path ices over in November.
Stop 2
Skógafoss ~30 min further
Wide accessible viewing platform for the full curtain of water. In rain — as the cast found — 5 minutes, sprint, 冲啊, back to the car. It works.
Stop 3
Vík century restaurant Lunch
Where the cast ate — a beloved local institution in the village and a well-earned warm stop mid-route.
After lunch
Vík church (Víkurkirkja) ~2 min drive up the hill · ~15 min
The red-roofed church on the hill above the village, with a view over Vík's rooftops and out to the Reynisdrangar sea stacks. Drive up and park beside it — only a few steps from the car. A quick wool-shop browse in the village fits here too.
Stop 4
Reynisfjara black sand beach ~10 min from Vík
Dramatic basalt columns, black sand, raw Atlantic. Stay well back from the waves at ALL times — sneaker waves here are genuinely dangerous.

Kirkjubæjarklaustur ~1h east of Vík
Check in to the cabin for three nights. Far from city lights — step outside after dark and look north for the aurora.

Nov17TueDay 7

Glacier lagoon & Diamond Beach — then decide: east to Höfn, or back west
🏨 Kirkjubæjarklaustur cabin (night 2 of 3) · 🚗 ~250 km round trip (west option) · ~420 km · ~5h 30min driving (east option)

Leave by 08:00 to reach Jökulsárlón around sunrise. After Diamond Beach, decide on the spot: if it is clear and everyone has energy, continue east to Höfn and Stokksnes; if it is windy, wet or people are tired, head back west via Fjallsárlón.

Breakfast
At the cabin ~07:00
An early breakfast before the 08:00 start. Fill a thermos and pack snacks for the car.
Morning
Jökulsárlón ~1h 45min from Klaustur · ~10:00–11:00
Icebergs drifting in total silence, seals resting on the floating ice — a flat walk along the shore from the car park.
Late morning
Diamond Beach Across the road · ~11:00–11:45
Ice chunks glittering on black sand.
East option
Höfn · Pakkhús ~1h east of Jökulsárlón · lunch ~12:45–13:45
Iceland's "langoustine capital" — lunch at Pakkhús by the harbour. Park, walk in, sit down: no real walking. In November the langoustine is usually frozen tails rather than fresh, so confirm it is on the menu when booking.
East option
Stokksnes / Vestrahorn ~15–20 min east of Höfn · ~14:05–15:30
The jagged Vestrahorn mountain over black-sand dunes, with a lighthouse and a replica Viking village film set. Entry ~1,100 ISK per person at the Viking Café (card-only machine in the car park out of hours). The main viewpoints are a few minutes from the car; the café is a warm place to wait. Very exposed to wind — ice cleats on. Sunset ~16:10, then ~2h 45min back to the cabin in the dark (arrive ~18:15).
West option
Fjallsárlón ~10 min west of Jökulsárlón, on the way back · lunch ~12:00–12:45, lagoon ~12:45–13:30
A smaller, quieter lagoon where the glacier comes right down to the water — a short walk from the car park. Have lunch here at Frost, the self-service restaurant (hot and cold dishes, soup, cake; listed as open daily 10:00–16:00 all year — confirm hours). Backup: the Jökulsárlón café before you leave (open year-round, ~9:00–17:00; lobster soup, sandwiches, cake). Back at the cabin by ~16:00. (If you go east today, Fjallsárlón moves to Day 8.)
Dinner
Hotel Klaustur restaurant ~19:00 · book ahead (cancellable)
Hearty Icelandic mains, listed as open year-round — so nobody has to cook after the long east option (back ~18:15). If you take the west option and are back by ~16:00, cook at the cabin or try Systrakaffi instead, and cancel the booking.
NatureGlacierFoodSelf-drive
Fill up before leaving Klaustur — stations are few and far between on this stretch (refuel in Höfn if going east). Check umferdin.is and the weather at vedur.is before setting off. The east option means a long return drive in darkness — only go if the driver is comfortable with night driving on the Ring Road.
East-or-west check — the weather decides, not opening hours (Stokksnes is open year-round). Check three times: the night before at the cabin, at 07:00 before leaving, and finally at ~11:45 at Diamond Beach (there is mobile signal at the lagoon). Go east only if ALL of these pass: (1) no yellow/orange/red weather warning for Southeast Iceland this afternoon or evening — en.vedur.is; (2) wind gusts below ~20 m/s from 14:00 to 19:00 on the vedur.is forecasts for Höfn and Kirkjubæjarklaustur — the Öræfi stretch near Skaftafell is known for strong crosswinds; (3) Route 1 from Höfn to Klaustur shown as easily passable, with no ice, snow or closures — umferdin.is/en (also has road webcams); (4) no South or Southeast coast alerts on safetravel.is; (5) Vestrahorn not buried in low cloud (vedur.is cloud-cover map); (6) Pakkhús confirmed open with your table (+354 478 2280). Golden Circle
Golden Circle loop — at an easy pace
🏨 Amtmann Residence, Selfoss (night 2 of 2) · 🚗 ~200 km round trip

The Golden Circle is the same route 迪丽热巴 guided on the cast's first day in Iceland (Ep 10). Run the loop in the classic direction — Þingvellir first, then north to Geysir and Gullfoss, then back down to Friðheimar for a late lunch. Leave by 09:15 to reach Þingvellir as the light comes up. Kerið is saved for tomorrow morning, on the way to Reykjavík, where it will catch far better light than it would at dusk today.

Breakfast
At the apartment before the 09:15 start
Pack snacks for the car — lunch at Friðheimar is late (~14:15), so plan a mid-morning snack or coffee at Geysir.
Stop 1
Þingvellir National Park ~45 min from Selfoss
Iceland's most historically significant site — the world's oldest parliament (Alþingi, founded 930 AD) was held here on these flat lava plains. The site also sits directly on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge: the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates are visibly pulling apart. Silfra Fissure is here too — the cast's snorkelling highlight. You're skipping the snorkel, but the rift valley itself is spectacular to walk along. Allow ~1h 15min.
Stop 2
Geysir — Strokkur geyser ~50 min from Þingvellir
Located in the Haukadalur geothermal valley. The famous Great Geysir rarely erupts anymore — but Strokkur beside it is one of the most reliably active geysers in the world, erupting 20–40m every 5–10 minutes. The cast timed creative group shots to each eruption. The 澎湃 guide called the Great Geysir 'the earth's tears' — usually mirror-still, then a few seconds of trembling, then a sudden burst of power.
Stop 3
Gullfoss — the Golden Waterfall ~10 min from Geysir
Listed among the world's top-10 waterfalls — glacial meltwater from Langjökull plunging into a dramatic two-tier gorge. Stay on the upper viewing platform, which is level and step-free; the lower path to the water's edge involves uneven steps and is best skipped in November. Spray can freeze on the railings, so wear grip gloves.
Stop 4
Friðheimar ~30 min from Gullfoss · Lunch
Lunch inside a working tomato greenhouse — the famous tomato soup with fresh-baked bread, surrounded by tomato vines. Warm, indoor and level underfoot: a good place to end the day. Book ahead for ~14:00; if you would rather eat earlier, book ~12:45 and visit Gullfoss after lunch instead.
Dinner
Light dinner in Selfoss after the late lunch
Keep it light after Friðheimar: Kaffi Krús (Austurvegur 7 — burgers, pizza, café food; same owners as Tryggvaskáli), Tommi's Burger Joint (Eyravegur 32, listed 11:00–21:00), or something simple at the apartment.

Cards must show a Visa, Plus, Mastercard, Cirrus or Maestro logo on the back — NETS-only ATM cards won't work overseas. Turn on overseas use and ATM withdrawals in each bank's app (Trust, UOB TMRW, POSB/DBS digibank) for Nov 11–24, and confirm each 6-digit PIN. Use the Trust debit card (savings account) for cash — not the Trust credit card, which charges cash-advance fees. UOB / POSB debit cards also work but cost ~S$5 + ~3% per withdrawal. Carry a backup card on a different network. In Iceland use bank ATMs (hraðbanki): Landsbankinn, Arion banki or Íslandsbanki — avoid Euronet ATMs. Always decline conversion to SGD and choose ISK. Withdraw ~ISK 10,000 at KEF arrivals on Nov 12 (see Day 2).
