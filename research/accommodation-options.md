## Takeaways

- **Airbnb is cheaper at all three stops.** It saves roughly $150–450/night compared with the hotels still available.
- **Kirkjubæjarklaustur is the most urgent.** Hótel Klaustur and Hótel Laki are gone on Agoda, and Núpar has its last 2 rooms. Book either Núpar or one of the Airbnbs soon. Hótel Klaustur's own website ([hotelklaustur.is](https://hotelklaustur.is/)) may still have rooms that Agoda doesn't.
- **Reykjavík:** if you prefer a hotel, the Sand Hotel suite that fits all 4 ($557 with breakfast) is the best value. The Historical townhouse on Airbnb is cheapest.
- **Hotel Rangá** now costs about $1,000/room/night. Hótel Selfoss or an Airbnb with a hot tub is a much cheaper way to watch for the aurora.

## About these prices

- Agoda searched as 4 adults, so rooms that allow children free (Hotel Klaustur, for example) may come out cheaper.
- Reykjavik Residence was not checked because Agoda's page for it didn't load.
- Before booking an Airbnb, check where exactly it is. For the Klaustur and Hella stays, also check that the access road is plowed in November. For downtown Reykjavík, check how far it is to walk from the KEF airport-bus drop-off.
