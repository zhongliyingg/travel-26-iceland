# Accommodation Options — Outstanding Stays

Airbnb vs hotels for the three stays still to book. Prices captured **20 Sep 2026**, in USD, and will change.

- **All prices are per night for the whole family of 4.**
- **Hotels:** live Agoda rates, taxes included, searched as 2 rooms / 4 adults. Agoda quotes per room, so where 2 rooms are needed the price is doubled.
- **Airbnb:** the total price Airbnb showed for the stay (whole homes only, searched as 2 adults + 2 children), divided by the number of nights.

> ⚠️ **Two constraints now outrank price.**
>
> 1. **Two parents aged 70+** — accessibility matters. But the real filter is *loft ladders, steep stairs and unplowed remote tracks*, **not** "Airbnb" as a category. A single-level apartment with plowed access is fine.
> 2. **Asian diet / self-catering.** Icelandic hotel rooms essentially never have kitchens — a kettle is the ceiling. **Choosing a hotel rules out cooking for that stay.** The value of a kitchen varies enormously by stay; see [Food & self-catering](#food--self-catering).
>
> These pull in opposite directions, and **apartments / aparthotels resolve both** — lift, reception and cleared parking *plus* a kitchen. That combination is under-represented in the tables below and is worth hunting for.
>
> The **Check before booking** column flags **category-level risks inferred from the listing type** — "cabin", "cottage", "townhouse", "secluded". They are **not verified facts about individual properties**; none of these listings has been inspected. Treat each one as a question to ask the host or hotel, not as a finding.

## Takeaways

**Revised 20 Sep 2026**, after the Days 9–11 restructure, the 70+ accessibility constraint and the self-catering question.

**Don't pick one model for the whole trip — split it by stay.** A blanket "all hotels" plan rules out cooking for all 10 Iceland nights; a blanket "all Airbnb" plan takes on stair and access risk where it buys nothing. The value of a kitchen is wildly uneven across the three stays, so match the choice to the stay:

| Stay | Nights | Kitchen value | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reykjavík | 4 | Low — best Asian eating of the trip | **Hotel** (Sand Hotel), or an aparthotel if you want the option |
| **Klaustur** | 3 | **Highest** — restaurants open but all Icelandic-Western | **Self-catering**, filtered for single-level + plowed access |
| Selfoss | 2 | Medium — proper town, Krónan supermarket | **Hotel** (Hótel Selfoss) |

- **Klaustur is where the food argument wins, and it's also the most urgent booking.** Hótel Klaustur and Hótel Laki are gone on Agoda; Núpar was down to its last 2 rooms. Rather than defaulting to **Fosshótel Núpar w/ breakfast ($630)**, re-run the Airbnb shortlist with a single-level filter and message hosts with two questions: *any loft bedrooms, and is the track plowed in November?* Kársstaðir (4.99) and Country Home in Nature are the ones to ask first. - **Hella / Selfoss → Hótel Selfoss ($640).** Wins on both axes: ~35–40 min less driving than Hella under the revised order, and less than a third of Rangá's $2,014 for two rooms. Two nights in a town — a kitchen adds little.
- **Rangá's aurora wake-up call is worth less than it looks.** The dark-sky nights are Nov 16–18 at Klaustur, which the itinerary already flags. Hella and Selfoss both sit nearer Reykjavík's light dome, so the service applies on the two weakest aurora nights of the trip.

## Food & self-catering

**The constraint:** Icelandic hotel rooms don't have kitchens. Booking a hotel for a stay means eating out every night of it.

**Stock-up plan.** [Mai Thai](https://www.maithai.is/) on Laugavegur (by Hlemmur) is a Thai restaurant with a Thai supermarket at the back — rice, noodles, sauces, condiments, fresh Asian veg. Dai Phat is the other option. Both are in Reykjavík and there is **nothing comparable once you leave on Nov 16**, so buy the whole trip's supplies during the Nov 12–15 stay. Mai Thai sits on Laugavegur, already on the Day 3 and Day 5 walking routes.

**Rural shopping.** Klaustur has a small Kjörbúðin/Samkaup for basics; Selfoss has a full Krónan; Vík has a supermarket on the Day 6 and Day 8 routes. Expect Icelandic staples only — no Asian ingredients.

**Supermarket hours bite in November.** Bónus typically closes around 18:30; Krónan runs later. With sunset at ~16:10 and dark drives back, shop *before* the day's driving or on the way through, not after returning.

**Even on a pure-hotel plan, pack a travel kettle.** Instant noodles, congee packets and tea cover breakfasts and late arrivals, and most hotel rooms have a kettle anyway.

**Bringing food in.** Iceland allows a personal food allowance (commonly cited as up to 3 kg, excluding raw meat, raw dairy and raw eggs), so shelf-stable noodles, instant congee, vacuum-packed items and chili crisp are generally fine to bring from Singapore. ⚠️ Confirm the current rule before packing — this has not been verified against Icelandic customs for 2026.

## Accessibility — what to verify before booking

Ask every one of these. A "yes" on price does not survive a "no" on stairs.

**Any property**
1. Is there a **lift**, or is there a bedroom and full bathroom on the **entrance level**?
2. **Walk-in shower or bathtub?** Climbing into a tub is a common fall point.
3. How far is **parking to the door**, and is that path **lit and cleared** in November?
4. Is there **breakfast on site** — or, if self-catering, a shop nearby for the night before an 08:30 start?

**Airbnb specifically**
5. Are any bedrooms a **loft reached by ladder or open stair**? Very common in Icelandic cabins and rarely obvious from photos.
6. Is the **access road plowed** through November, and is it paved or gravel?
7. Is anyone **reachable at night** if something goes wrong?
8. If there's a hot tub: is it reached by **steps or decking**, and is that surface gritted?

**Kitchen (self-catering stays)**
9. Is there a **proper hob and oven**, or only a microwave and kettle? Listings say "kitchen" for both.
10. Is there a **rice cooker**, or at least a lidded saucepan? Worth asking — some Icelandic self-catering places have one.
11. Are **basic oils, salt and soy** provided, or is it bare? Affects how much you carry from Reykjavík.
12. How far is the **nearest supermarket**, and what are its November hours?

**Hotels specifically**
13. Are both rooms in the **main building**, or in an annexe reached by an outdoor path? (Directly relevant to Fosshótel Núpar and Magma.)
14. Can you request rooms **near the lift or reception** at booking?
